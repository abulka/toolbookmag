Toolbook Resources

There are many useful Toolbook resources - primarily internet based.

There are approximately 20 Toolbook Websites.  See the reviews in this Toolbook Tips Magazine.

There are 2 Toolbook discussions forums on the internet.  For instructions on getting on them, see the section on 'Internet Newsgroups' in this Toolbook Tips Magazine.  

There used to be many years of Compuserve hosted Toolbook discussion forums, but this ended in 1997.  You can search some of these forums online.  You can even download the forums databases and read them offline - see Toolbook Knowledge Nuggets 

There is also a knowledge base available for Toolbook. The Asymetrix knowledge base (online and downloadable) contains articles added by Asymetrix tech support staff and Toolbook engineers.

To access thousands more tips offline - download Toolbook Knowledge Nuggets
