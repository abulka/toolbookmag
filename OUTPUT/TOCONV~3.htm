Resources

A Toolbook tip can come from anywhere - your own experience, or from what you discover reading the online Toolbook discussion forums.

Another resource you can freely use is the Asymetrix Knowledge Base – in both its online and downloadable versions. See 

http://www.asymetrix.com

Yet another useful resource put out by the Australian Toolbook User Group is the downloadable Toolbook Knowledge Nuggets software – a powerful offline, discussion browser, with built in "tip making" features. More information is available from: 

http://www.atug.com/nuggets.htm


Animated GIF screen captures

For creating animated GIFS – I recommend and use the Gif Gif Gif software available from Pedagoguery Software at 

http://www.cafe.net/peda/

Email in you tip in word format, and please include a GIF or animated GIF illustrating your tip. Send to
